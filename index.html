The Framework

Every deal follows the same map.
MEDDPICC is how I navigate it.

MEDDPICC isn't just a qualification checklist — it's a deal operating system. I use it to structure every outbound motion: from initial research to pipeline management to closed-won. Here's how each element drives my GTM approach.

M
Metrics
Quantify the business impact upfront. I lead with ROI-driven messaging — revenue gained, costs saved, time recovered — so prospects see the value before the pitch.
E
Economic Buyer
Identify and target the person who signs the check. My outbound targets C-suite and VP-level decision makers — not gatekeepers.
D
Decision Criteria
Understand what matters to them. I research company priorities, tech stack, and pain points to align outreach with their evaluation framework.
D
Decision Process
Map the buying journey. Multi-stakeholder sequences are timed to match procurement cycles, budget windows, and internal approval flows.
P
Paper Process
Anticipate procurement friction. I surface legal, security, and compliance requirements early so deals don't stall at the finish line.
I
Identify Pain
Lead with the problem, not the product. Signal-based research (funding, hiring surges, leadership changes) reveals pain points before the first message.
C
Champion
Find the internal advocate. Multi-threading into accounts lets me identify and enable the person who will sell internally on our behalf.
C
Competition
Know the landscape. I track competitor positioning, recent switches, and contract timelines to time outreach when prospects are most open to change.
